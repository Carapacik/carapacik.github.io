---
title: "Splat"
description: "An oral care app with personalized recommendations, test strip analysis, and a built-in shop."
projectType: "Mobile app"
---

Questionnaires about oral health and daily habits help tailor personal care recommendations. The app uses the smartphone camera and OpenCV to analyze test strips for oral bacteria.

The built-in shop lets users choose oral care products, pay for orders, and arrange delivery to Yandex and CDEK pickup points shown on a Yandex MapKit map. Animations accompany questionnaires, screen transitions, and other interactions.
