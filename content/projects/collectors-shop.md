title: "Collectors shop"
description: "A store for collectible Dota 2 sets and skins on Android, iOS, and Telegram, with a searchable catalog and in-app ordering."
projectType: "Mobile app · Telegram Mini App"
---

Browse sets from Collector's Cache and other Dota 2 collections, explore item details, and add your favorites to the cart. The app brings catalog search and filters, promo codes, checkout, and order tracking together in one place.

Available on Google Play and the App Store, and as a Telegram Mini App that opens directly in the messenger without a separate installation.

Built with Flutter and Dart, using BLoC for state management.
