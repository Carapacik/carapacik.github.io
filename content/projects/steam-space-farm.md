title: "SteamSpaceFarm"
description: "Manage your Steam library, farm trading cards, and reach playtime goals with automated sessions and Steam Family support."
projectType: "Multiplatform desktop app"
---

A desktop app for Windows, macOS, and Linux that brings your own games and Steam Family library into one catalog. Search by name, AppID, or Steam link, filter and sort games, and keep favorites close at hand.

Farm trading cards in three modes, run up to 31 apps at once or use a sequential queue, and set playtime targets with automatic stopping. Card farming takes priority; queued playtime sessions resume afterwards. Sessions can run through the Steam client or independently via SteamKit.

Sign in with a QR code or Steam Guard, choose from four library card styles, and switch between English, Russian, Spanish, and Simplified Chinese. The app combines a Flutter interface with .NET helpers for Steam integration.
