title: "SteamSpaceFarm"
description: "Manage your Steam library and simulated game sessions with playtime targets, queues, and Steam Family support."
projectType: "Multiplatform desktop app"
---

Browse and filter your library, set playtime goals, and manage session queues through the Steam client. The app supports Windows, Linux, and macOS, combining a Flutter interface with .NET helpers for Steam integration.
